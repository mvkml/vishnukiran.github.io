Anti-Corruption Layer

DDD Strategic PatternPlanned
Plain: An Anti-Corruption Layer protects one domain from being distorted by another domain's model.

What It Is

Anti-Corruption Layer comes from Domain-Driven Design, notably Eric Evans' DDD work. It translates between bounded contexts so one model does not leak into another.

Why, When, How

Use it when integrating systems with different language, rules, ownership, or data shapes.

Why We Chose It Here

ICU concepts should not force outpatient models to change, and billing or insurance concepts should not leak into clinical tool contracts unless explicitly mapped.
