Facade

GoF Structural PatternPlanned
Plain: A Facade gives a simple interface over a complicated subsystem.

What It Is

Facade is a classic structural design pattern from the Gang of Four catalog. It hides subsystem complexity behind a smaller, easier entry point.

Why, When, How

Use it when callers should not understand every internal component, dependency, or ordering rule needed to complete a workflow.

Why We Chose It Here

Multiple MCP hosts can become complex to configure. A facade can present a simple Doctor tool surface while the underlying implementation still respects host isolation.
