Service Discovery

Infrastructure PatternPlanned
Plain: Service Discovery lets clients find where a service is running without hardcoding every address.

What It Is

Service Discovery is common in cloud-native and microservice platforms. Services register their location, and callers resolve the current endpoint at runtime.

Why, When, How

Use it when services scale, move, restart, or deploy independently across environments.

Why We Chose It Here

Separate MCP hosts may run on different ports, containers, or cloud services. The gateway or BFF should discover approved hosts rather than hardcode brittle URLs everywhere.
